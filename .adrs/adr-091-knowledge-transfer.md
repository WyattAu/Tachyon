# ADR-091: Knowledge Transfer Strategy

## Status

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-02-12  
**Decision Date:** 2026-02-12  

## Context

The Tachyon project has accumulated significant knowledge, patterns, and best practices. Effective knowledge transfer to team members, stakeholders, and future projects is critical for maximizing project value.

## Problem

How do we ensure that project knowledge is effectively transferred to all relevant stakeholders, including team members, new hires, and future project teams?

## Decision

### Knowledge Transfer Framework

The Tachyon project adopts a comprehensive knowledge transfer framework:

1. **Knowledge Inventory:** Comprehensive catalog of all project knowledge
2. **Transfer Channels:** Multiple channels for knowledge dissemination
3. **Documentation:** Well-organized and accessible documentation
4. **Training:** Structured training programs
5. **Mentorship:** Peer-to-peer knowledge sharing
6. **Review and Feedback:** Continuous improvement of transfer effectiveness

## Consequences

### Positive Consequences

- Improved team capability and productivity
- Reduced onboarding time for new team members
- Preservation of institutional knowledge
- Better decision-making based on past experience
- Reduced risk of knowledge loss

### Negative Consequences

- Time required for knowledge transfer activities
- Potential knowledge gaps if transfer incomplete
- Risk of knowledge becoming outdated
- Requires ongoing maintenance

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Documentation Only:** Rejected due to passive transfer
2. **Informal Knowledge Sharing:** Rejected due to lack of structure
3. **External Training:** Rejected due to project-specific knowledge

## Implementation

### Transfer Process

1. **Knowledge Inventory:** Catalog all project knowledge
2. **Transfer Planning:** Plan transfer activities by audience
3. **Transfer Execution:** Execute transfer activities
4. **Transfer Assessment:** Evaluate transfer effectiveness
5. **Knowledge Maintenance:** Keep knowledge up to date

### Transfer Methods

#### Documentation

- **Technical Documentation:** Architecture, design, implementation
- **Process Documentation:** Development, testing, operations
- **Knowledge Base:** Patterns, anti-patterns, lessons learned
- **API Documentation:** Complete API reference
- **User Documentation:** Guides, tutorials, FAQs

#### Training

- **Onboarding Training:** New team member onboarding
- **Technical Training:** Deep dives into architecture and implementation
- **Process Training:** Development and testing processes
- **Tool Training:** Tools and frameworks
- **Best Practices:** Pattern and anti-pattern training

#### Mentorship

- **Technical Mentorship:** Senior to junior developer pairing
- **Process Mentorship:** Experienced team members guiding others
- **Cross-Team Mentorship:** Knowledge sharing across teams
- **Domain Expertise:** Subject matter expert consultation

#### Hands-On Experience

- **Pair Programming:** Real-time knowledge transfer
- **Code Review:** Learning through review
- **Bug Triage:** Learning through problem solving
- **Prototype Development:** Learning through exploration
